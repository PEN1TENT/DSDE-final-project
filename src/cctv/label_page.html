Counting rules
  • Count every vehicle you can identify, even if partly hidden or cut off at the edge.
  • Car: sedans, pickups, vans, taxis, SUVs, tuk-tuks, songthaews.
  • Truck: large goods vehicles only (6+ wheels).
  • Motorcycle: one per bike, not per rider.
  • At night, count a pair of lights only if you are sure it is one vehicle.
  • Mark unusable if the picture is blank, frozen or too foggy to count.
Keys: letter adds 1, Shift+letter removes 1, ←/→ move.
